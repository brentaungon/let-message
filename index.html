A letter before tomorrow

For You

Hello My!

Tomorrow is your day.

I know ga andam gid ka for a long time, and I know there were days when you felt tired, scared, or unsure sa imong kaugalingon. But please remember how far you’ve already come. You made it this far for a reason.

Bisan dili kaayo ta magkasinabtanay, know that I’m always rooting for you dili lang for tomorrow, but for everything you’re facing and every dream you’re chasing in life. I may not always be there sa imong tapad, and I may not always know the right words nga e storya, but quietly, I’ll always wish the best for you.

I care about you, I believe in you, and I love you. Even from a distance, I’ll always be cheering for you.

Dili ko mo add ug pressure or expectations. I just want you to walk into that room with a calm heart, trust everything you’ve learned, and give it your best.

Good luck on your LET tomorrow. I’m proud of you, more than you probably realize. Whatever happens, I hope you know that somewhere out here, there’s a heart that will always be rooting for you. 🤍 Halong ka dira, I LOVE YOU! 🤍

Always rooting for you. 🤍
~ Daddy 🤍

Whatever tomorrow brings, remember this.

You don't have to be perfect. Just believe in yourself—the person who, bisan unsa pay naagian, made it this far.

And when the room gets quiet, when the questions get difficult, and when doubt starts whispering again— remember that someone, somewhere, is quietly praying that you make it through.

🤍
Always rooting for you.

Some feelings are meant to linger quietly, understood without ever needing to be named.
