Tomorrow is your day.

I know you’ve been preparing for this for a long time, and I know there were days when you felt tired, scared, or unsure of yourself. But please remember how far you’ve already come. You made it this far for a reason.

Bisag dili kaayo ta magkasinabtanay, know that I’m always rooting for you—not just for tomorrow, but for everything you’re facing and every dream you’re chasing in life. I may not always be there beside you, and I may not always know the right words to say, but quietly, I’ll always wish the best for you.

I don’t want to add pressure or expectations. I just want you to walk into that room with a calm heart, trust everything you’ve learned, and give it your best.

And if you ever wonder who is still wishing for your success from a distance… maybe you already know the answer.

Good luck on your LET tomorrow.

I’m proud of you, more than you probably realize.

Whatever happens, I hope you know that somewhere out here, there’s a heart that will always be rooting for you.
